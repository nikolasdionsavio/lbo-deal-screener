# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PE/IB analysts, recruiters, and finance students evaluating the author's engineering and
finance judgment, plus the author himself running real screens. Context: desktop, daytime
office light or late-night laptop; skimming fast, judging credibility in the first ten
seconds, then reading dense numbers carefully. The job: screen a public company as an LBO
candidate and trust every figure shown.

## Product Purpose

A PE-style deal screening platform for public companies: traceable KPIs, valuation,
a 5-year LBO model with sensitivities, peer comps, deal score, auto-generated memo,
filings, and news. Success = a finance professional says "this looks like an internal
fund tool, and the numbers check out." It is a portfolio piece whose credibility IS the
product; it must never look AI-generated or gimmicky.

## Brand Personality

Institutional, precise, calm. The voice of a diligence memo: declarative sentences,
numbers carry the emotion. Confidence through restraint, never through decoration.

## Anti-references

- SaaS-cream landing pages, gradient text, hero-metric templates.
- Retail-investor apps (Robinhood confetti energy) and crypto-dashboard neon.
- Anything that reads "AI-generated template": uniform card grids, eyebrow labels on
  every section, decorative emoji, hype copy.

## Materials

Translucent glass is a primary surface style (2026). It is the material for
things that FLOAT: chrome, drawers, menus, sticky rails, the search field. It
is not a decorative treatment applied to content blocks, and it never goes on a
data table. See DESIGN.md "Glass materials" for the tiers and the rules that
keep it readable.

## Design Principles

1. **Numbers first.** Typography, alignment (right-aligned, tabular-nums), and spacing
   exist to make dense figures scannable. Decoration that competes with data loses.
2. **Traceable, never black-box.** Formulas, sources, dates, and warnings are visible
   design elements, not footnotes to hide.
3. **Quiet surfaces, deliberate accents.** One accent earns attention (positive/CTA);
   red only for true negatives; amber only for warnings. Everything else stays neutral.
4. **Bank-grade restraint, executed with craft.** The upgrade path is better type,
   rhythm, and detail, not more color or motion.
5. **Honest states.** Loading, missing data, and degraded sources look designed, not
   apologetic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1 in both themes, focus states visible, reduced-motion honored,
color never the sole carrier of meaning (deltas get signs, warnings get text).
